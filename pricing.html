Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to commit to a long contract?

No. Monthly packages are month-to-month. We offer discounts for 3, 6, or 12 month commitments, but you're never locked in. Just give us 30 days notice if you want to stop.

What if I don't like the content?

Every package includes 2 rounds of revisions per post. If something doesn't feel right, tell us and we'll fix it. We also do a content calendar review before we start creating, so you can approve the direction early.

Do you handle the photoshoots yourself?

We coordinate with freelance photographers in Delhi NCR. We brief them, schedule the shoot at your location, and handle the editing. You just need to be there and have your products/space ready.

Can I switch packages later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle.

What if I just need a website, no social media?

No problem. Website projects are one-time and don't require a monthly package. Check our one-time services above.

How do you communicate with clients?

WhatsApp is our primary channel. Quick, easy, and everyone already has it. We also do monthly strategy calls (Google Meet or phone).

Ready to get started?

Pick a package or send us a message. We'll figure out the best plan for your business together.
