E-commerce 2025 - Ongoing

Happy Fashion Atelier

Noida Sector 18, Delhi NCR

The Business

A boutique handbag and fashion accessories store in Noida's busiest market. Great products, loyal walk-in customers, but zero digital presence. No website, no active social media, no way to reach customers beyond the local foot traffic.

The Challenge

Happy Fashion wanted to sell online and attract younger customers through Instagram. They also needed a better way to handle customer inquiries on WhatsApp — too many messages were getting lost or replied to late.

What We Did

  • Built a complete Shopify e-commerce storefront for handbags
  • Set up product catalog with photos and descriptions
  • Integrated payment gateway for online transactions
  • Created Instagram content strategy and growth plan
  • Set up WhatsApp Business API with auto-reply workflows
  • Ongoing support and maintenance

The Results

Online Store Live
24/7 selling capability
Instagram Active
Regular content + growth
WhatsApp Automated
Faster customer responses
New Sales Channel
Beyond walk-in traffic
